  2. Customize Design

    Adjust colors, gradients, eyes, dots, size, margin and upload a logo for brand consistency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data sent to third parties?

Only the content you explicitly send to the backend for AI generation or download is transmitted. API keys are stored on the backend only.

Can I use my own logo?

Yes — upload a transparent PNG or SVG and adjust the logo size and placement in the studio.

What QR code formats are available?

We support PNG exports and plan to add SVG/vector exports for print-ready assets.

How does AI improve my QR codes?

AI provides theme and copy suggestions, accessibility recommendations, and content tailored to your use case, speeding up design and improving engagement.
